Cookie policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

This is an English translation for information. The Spanish version is the legally binding one.

This site stores very little information in your browser, and nearly all of it is there so that what you use works. This is what there is, under article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE and the cookie guide of the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

What is always stored (necessary)

Your permission is not needed because without this what you ask for would not work. It is not used to track you.

Necessary data stored by the site
NameTypeWhat it is forHow long it lasts
thv_motionSession storageRemembering whether you asked to see the site with or without animations.Until you close the tab
thv-compra-…Local storageWhat you tick on each recipe’s shopping list.Until you click “Untick everything” or clear your browser data
thv_consentLocal storageYour analytics choice, so we do not ask you again.Until you change it or clear your browser data
thv_pasaporteLocal storageThe stamps in your passport: which recipes you have stamped and when.Until you click “Delete the stamps” or clear your browser data

What is only stored if you accept it (analytics)

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 (from Google Ireland Limited) sets these cookies to count visits overall. If you reject it or do not choose, nothing from Google is loaded.

Analytics cookies
NameTypeWhat it is forHow long it lasts
_gaGoogle cookieTelling visits apart anonymously in order to count them.2 years
_ga_…Google cookieKeeping the state of the analytics session.2 years

The newsletter and Rumbo

The newsletter form sends your email directly to MailerLite without loading any of its software on the site, so it does not set cookies. Rumbo, the site’s AI, does not store anything in your browser either: the conversation disappears when you close the page.

How to change your mind

You can change your choice at any time with the button below or with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. You can also delete this data in your browser settings.
