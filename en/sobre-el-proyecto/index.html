From the kitchen to the journey

Tengo Hambre Viajera (“I’m hungry, traveller”) brings together, in each page, a recipe you can make at home and a place worth discovering. It starts with the Basque Country and Portugal, and the table will keep adding sweet and savoury dishes from all over the world.

How each recipe is prepared

Each recipe gives the servings, the times, the ingredients with their quantities, a shopping list and the steps. Quantities and steps are checked against the original source, which appears at the end of each recipe. If there is a well-documented fine-dining version, it is offered as an option with its own steps.

The images

The images of dishes and places are illustrative: they help you recognise the dish and the place, but they are not photographs taken there.
