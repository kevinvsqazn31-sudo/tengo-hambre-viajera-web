Ingredients

  • 1 kgpotatoes
  • 2chorizo sausages
  • 1onion
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • 1red pepper
  • bay leaf and thyme, to taste
  • olive oil, a little
  • salt, to taste
  • water, enough to cover

Cooking mode

Step by step

  1. 1

    Wash and peel the potatoes and break them into rough, uneven chunks.

  2. 2

    Wash the pepper, remove the seeds and cut it into strips. Finely chop the onion and cut the garlic cloves in half to remove the germ.

  3. 3

    In the stew pot, brown the garlic in a little olive oil. When it takes on colour, add the onion and the pepper and fry them until they start to brown.

  4. 4

    Add the chorizo sausages cut in half, four pieces in all, and let the base take on a nice reddish colour.

  5. 5

    Add the potatoes with the bay leaf and thyme and stir so they soak up the flavour. Cover them with water and bring quickly to the boil.

  6. 6

    Lower the heat and let them cook over a medium, rather lively heat: if a potato breaks up, that is fine, it thickens the broth.

  7. 7

    Taste for salt and let it rest for 15 minutes before serving.

Tips

  • It should be a creamy stew, without too much water. If needed, crush a potato or two to thicken it.
  • The City Council recipe includes bay leaf and thyme but does not say when to add them; here they go in with the potatoes.

Have you cooked it?

Give your passport the Vitoria-Gasteiz stamp.

The dish

Vitoria-Gasteiz City Council lists it as one of the city's traditional recipes.

In Ali, on the outskirts of Vitoria-Gasteiz, the World Patatas con Chorizo Championship has been held for more than a decade.

How to order and serve it

Let it rest for about 15 minutes before serving: the broth thickens and the potato takes on the flavour of the chorizo.

Quick questions

What is patatas con chorizo?
The spoon stew of Vitoria-Gasteiz: potatoes broken into rough chunks, chorizo and a base of garlic, onion and red pepper that turns the broth red.
How long does the recipe take?
Between 50 minutes and 1 hour. The recipe serves 4 people and you can change the servings in the recipe.
Which allergens does it contain?
None of the recipe's ingredients contains any of the 14 allergens in itself. Check the label of: chorizo sausages (some contain milk (lactose), soya or gluten). The list is based on the ingredients, not on specific brands.
Where is it from?
From Basque Country (Spain). Its postcard: Santa María Cathedral, in Vitoria-Gasteiz.

About this place

It is the Gothic cathedral of Vitoria-Gasteiz: a 13th-century church with a Latin cross plan, a 14th-century portico and a tower about sixty metres high, the tallest in the city.

It became a cathedral in 1862. In 1994 it had to close because of structural problems, and its restoration can be visited through the “Open for restoration” programme, with guided tours of the works.

It has been a World Heritage Site since 2015, as part of the Northern Way of Saint James.

Address: Cantón de Santa María, 3. 01001 Vitoria-Gasteiz
