The recipe

Servings
4 people

Ingredients

  • 350 gdesalted salt cod, flaked
  • 125 gfried matchstick potatoes
  • 4eggs
  • 3egg yolks
  • 1onion, sliced into half moons
  • 3 clovesgarlic, chopped
  • 1 leafbay leaf
  • 4 tablespoonsolive oil
  • 1 teaspoonsalt
  • pepper, to taste
  • chopped parsley, to taste
  • black olives, to taste

Allergens

  • Egg in eggs and egg yolks
  • Fish in desalted salt cod, flaked

Check the label of:

  • fried matchstick potatoes: may contain traces of gluten depending on the brand.

This list is based on the recipe's ingredients, following the 14 allergens in Regulation (EU) 1169/2011, not on specific brands. If you have an allergy or intolerance, always check the labels of what you buy.

Cooking mode

One step at a time, in big letters, with timers and the screen always on.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a deep frying pan and gently fry the onion with the bay leaf.

  2. 2

    Add the garlic and stir. Add the flaked cod, press it down and mix it well with the onion.

  3. 3

    Add about 75 g of the matchstick potatoes and mix.

  4. 4

    Beat the eggs and the yolks in a bowl with the salt and pepper.

  5. 5

    Remove the bay leaf, pour in the egg and mix well. If it is too runny, leave it on the heat a little longer. Add the rest of the matchstick potatoes and fold them in.

  6. 6

    Just before serving, add the chopped parsley and the olives.

Tips

  • Do not let the egg set or dry out too much: it should stay creamy.

The brief cites the Salt Cod Centre (Centro del Bacalao) as the source. It does not publish its recipe online, so it still has to be checked against it.

The dish

It is one of the most widespread salt cod dishes in Portuguese cooking.

How to order and serve it

It should arrive juicy: the egg creamy, not dry, and the matchstick potatoes still a little crunchy.

Quick questions

What is bacalhau à Brás?
Flaked salt cod with onion, matchstick potatoes and barely set egg: a pan dish from Portuguese cooking finished with parsley and black olives.
How long does the recipe take?
30 minutes. The recipe serves 4 people and you can change the servings in the recipe.
Which allergens does it contain?
Contains egg (eggs and egg yolks) and fish (desalted salt cod, flaked). Check the label of: fried matchstick potatoes (may contain traces of gluten depending on the brand). The list is based on the ingredients, not on specific brands.
Where is it from?
From Portugal. Its postcard: Praça do Comércio, in Lisbon.

About this place

It is the great square of Lisbon's Baixa, facing the Tagus.

Its triumphal arch, the Rua Augusta arch, symbolises the rebirth of the city after the earthquake, fire and tsunami of 1755. Since 2013 you can climb up to its viewpoint.

Address: Rua Augusta, 2. 1100-053 Lisboa (Arco da Rua Augusta)
