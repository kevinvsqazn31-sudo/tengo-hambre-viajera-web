The recipe

4 people (about 30 small tarts)

Servings
4 people

Ingredients

Base

  • 1 packfresh puff pastry

Custard

  • 250 mlmilk
  • 125 mlwater
  • 175 gsugar
  • 25 gsifted wheat flour
  • 1 tablespoonsifted cornflour
  • 1lemon peel
  • 1 stickcinnamon stick
  • 5egg yolks

To serve

  • ground cinnamon, to taste

Allergens

  • Gluten in fresh puff pastry and sifted wheat flour
  • Egg in egg yolks
  • Milk in milk

Check the label of:

  • fresh puff pastry: all-butter pastry contains milk.
  • sifted cornflour: for coeliacs, it should say “gluten free”.

This list is based on the recipe's ingredients, following the 14 allergens in Regulation (EU) 1169/2011, not on specific brands. If you have an allergy or intolerance, always check the labels of what you buy.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 250 °C.

  2. 2

    Roll out the puff pastry on a floured worktop until it is a little thinner. Roll it up tightly along the long side and cut it into 30 equal pieces.

  3. 3

    Put each piece in a tart tin and, with wet fingers, press the dough out until it comes slightly over the rim. Set them aside.

  4. 4

    In a small saucepan, put the milk, the water, the cinnamon, the lemon peel, the sugar, the flour and the cornflour. Heat gently, stirring all the time, until it thickens.

  5. 5

    Pour a ladleful of that custard over the beaten yolks in a thin stream, stirring. Then tip the yolks into the saucepan and mix well.

  6. 6

    Remove the lemon peel and the cinnamon and leave to cool. Fill the tins two thirds full and bake straight away for 12 minutes.

  7. 7

    When you take them out of the oven, sprinkle them with a little cold water. Serve them warm, with ground cinnamon.

Tips

  • For a lactose-free version, swap the milk for an unsweetened rice or oat drink.

The dish

The pastel de nata is one of the great symbols of traditional Portuguese baking.

How to order and serve it

Have it warm and dusted with ground cinnamon, ideally with a coffee.

Quick questions

What is a pastel de nata?
One of the symbols of Portuguese baking: crisp puff pastry and a custard of egg yolk, lemon and cinnamon that a 250 °C oven leaves with toasted spots.
How long does the recipe take?
30 minutes. The recipe serves 4 people (about 30 small tarts) and you can change the servings in the recipe.
Which allergens does it contain?
Contains gluten (fresh puff pastry and sifted wheat flour), egg (egg yolks) and milk (milk). Check the label of: fresh puff pastry (all-butter pastry contains milk); sifted cornflour (for coeliacs, it should say “gluten free”). The list is based on the ingredients, not on specific brands.
Where is it from?
From Portugal. Its postcard: Belém Tower, in Lisbon.

About this place

A UNESCO World Heritage Site on the banks of the Tagus, it is the great icon of a country that grew by looking out to sea.

It was part of King John II's plan to defend Lisbon from enemy ships. It was finished in 1514 to a design by the architect Francisco de Arruda.

Manuel I wanted it to be a symbol of his reign: its stone carries the royal coat of arms, the armillary sphere and the cross of the Order of Christ.

Address: Av. Brasília, 1400-038 Lisboa
